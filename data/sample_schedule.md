# Sample Program Schedule

> Synthetic document used for the LLM Ops teaching session. Dates and times
> are illustrative and do not refer to any real cohort.

## Weekly Session Pattern

| Day | Time (IST) | Session type |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | 19:00 – 21:00 | Theory session |
| Saturday | 10:00 – 13:00 | Hands-on session |

Sessions run for 12 weeks, with a one-week break in week 7.

## Theory Sessions

Theory sessions introduce concepts and frameworks. Each session is structured as:

- 30 minutes — recap and motivation
- 60 minutes — core content delivery with worked examples
- 20 minutes — open Q&A
- 10 minutes — preview of the hands-on session

Theory sessions are recorded.

## Hands-on Sessions

Hands-on sessions are live coding labs. Participants work along with the
instructor and complete exercises in real time. Hands-on sessions are recorded,
but the experience is significantly better live — participants who attend
live can ask immediate questions.

## Industry Lecture

A 90-minute industry lecture is scheduled in **week 9**. The lecturer is a
practicing engineer or product leader from a partner organization. The
industry lecture replaces that week's theory session.

## Project Milestones

| Week | Milestone | Submission |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Project proposal | Friday 23:59 IST |
| 8 | Mid-project review | Friday 23:59 IST |
| 12 | Final project submission | Sunday 23:59 IST |
| 12 | Project presentation | Sunday during hands-on slot |

The project runs alongside weekly assignments. Project milestones are graded
separately from weekly assignments and contribute 40% of the final program
grade.

## Support Hours

Program Operations is available during:

- Monday to Friday, 09:00 – 18:00 IST (routine queries)
- Tuesday and Saturday session times (live support during sessions)
- Urgent-only on-call coverage outside these hours

## Holiday & Breaks

- **Week 7** — no live sessions; catch-up week for participants who need it
- Published public holidays may shift a session; any shift is announced in
  `#program-ops` at least one week in advance
