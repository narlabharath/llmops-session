# Sample Support Process

> Synthetic document used for the LLM Ops teaching session.

## When to Contact Support

Contact support when:

- You cannot access a session, recording, or assignment that should be available
- An emergency or personal situation will affect your participation
- You believe a grade has been recorded incorrectly
- You have a confirmed technical issue with the learning platform
- You need to raise an academic integrity concern

Do **not** contact support for:

- Asking the support team to bypass program policy on your behalf
- Routine "how do I" questions (try the FAQ first, then the peer channel)
- Requests for another participant's information

## Support Categories

| Category | Examples | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Technical | Platform errors, login issues, broken links | `#program-ops` with tag `technical` |
| Academic | Grade questions, rubric clarification | `#academic-help` |
| Urgent | Emergencies, medical situations affecting deadlines | `#program-ops` with tag `urgent` |
| Wellbeing | Personal situations affecting participation | DM to Program Operations |

## Technical Issue Process

1. Capture a screenshot of the issue if possible
2. Note the time and the action you were taking
3. Post in `#program-ops` with the `technical` tag, including the screenshot
4. Do **not** share login credentials or session tokens in the channel
5. A support engineer will respond within one business day

## Academic Doubt Process

1. Re-read the assignment brief and rubric
2. Check the FAQ document
3. If still unclear, post in `#academic-help` with a specific question
4. Avoid posting your draft solution publicly; if needed, share with the
   instructor by DM after they invite it

## Urgent Escalation Process

1. Post in `#program-ops` with the `urgent` tag and a one-line summary
2. Program Operations will acknowledge within four hours during business hours
3. For after-hours emergencies, DM the Program Operations on-call account
4. Documentation supporting an urgent request (e.g. medical note) may be
   requested but is handled confidentially

## Expected Response Time

| Channel/Type | Acknowledgement | Resolution attempt |
|---|---|---|
| `#program-ops` routine | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| `#program-ops` urgent | 4 business hours | Same/next day |
| `#academic-help` | 2 business days | Varies |
| `#peer-help` | Not SLA-bound | Not SLA-bound |

## What to Include in a Ticket

- A clear one-line summary
- What you were trying to do
- What actually happened
- What you have already tried
- Screenshots where useful
- A timestamp

## What Not to Share in Chat

- API keys, passwords, or session tokens
- Personal information about other participants
- Financial or identity documents (these are handled by DM only)
- Anything that you would not want a future cohort to see
