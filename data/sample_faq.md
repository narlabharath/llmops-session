# Sample Program FAQ

> Synthetic document used for the LLM Ops teaching session. For anything
> covered both here and in the **Program Policy**, the Policy is authoritative.

## Platform Access

**Q: How do I log in to the learning platform?**
Use the credentials emailed to you in your welcome message. If you have lost
the welcome email, raise a `technical` ticket per the support process.

**Q: I am locked out — what now?**
Use the platform's password reset flow first. If reset fails, raise a
`technical` ticket and include the email address you registered with.

## Joining Live Sessions

**Q: Where is the session link?**
Session links are posted in `#program-ops` 30 minutes before each session
starts. They are also visible in the schedule view inside the platform.

**Q: I joined late — can I still attend?**
Yes. Late joining is fine. Attendance is counted as long as you join within
the first 30 minutes of the session.

**Q: Can I attend on mobile?**
Theory sessions work fine on mobile. Hands-on sessions are not recommended
on mobile because you will need to code along.

## Accessing Recordings

**Q: When are recordings available?**
Within 24 hours of the session ending.

**Q: How long are recordings kept?**
For the duration of the program plus 90 days after program end. See the
**Program Policy** for the authoritative version.

**Q: Can I share recordings with a friend?**
No. Recordings are limited to the enrolled cohort.

## Assignment Submission Basics

**Q: Where do I submit?**
Through the submission form on the platform. Look for the assignment under
its week heading.

**Q: What file format?**
Whatever the assignment brief specifies. Default is `.ipynb` with outputs
saved for notebook assignments.

**Q: Can I edit my submission after submitting?**
You can re-submit any number of times before the deadline; the most recent
submission is the one that gets graded. After the deadline, see the late
submission policy.

## Peer Discussion Rules

**Q: Can I discuss assignments with peers?**
Discussing concepts is fine and encouraged. Sharing code or solutions is not
permitted unless the brief explicitly allows it. See the **Assignment
Guidelines** for the full rules.

## Asking Questions

**Q: Where should I post my question?**

- Administrative or scheduling → `#program-ops`
- Academic or technical-about-content → `#academic-help`
- Peer discussion → `#peer-help`

**Q: How fast will I get an answer?**
See the response-time table in the **Support Process** document.

## Support Response Time

**Q: I posted in #program-ops and haven't heard back. What now?**
Routine queries are answered within one business day. If you have not heard
back after one business day, reply to your own message tagging Program
Operations. For urgent issues, use the `urgent` tag.
