# Sample Program Policy

> Synthetic document used for the LLM Ops teaching session. Any resemblance
> to an actual training program is coincidental.

## Program Overview

The Sample GenAI Program is a 12-week part-time learning program covering
foundations of large language models, retrieval, evaluation, and operations.
Participants attend two live sessions per week and complete weekly hands-on
assignments. All program content, schedules, and policies are coordinated by
the Program Operations team.

## Attendance Expectations

Participants are expected to attend a minimum of **80% of live sessions**.
Attendance is tracked automatically by the learning platform.

- Participants who fall below the 80% threshold receive an email from
  Program Operations and are invited to a recovery plan conversation.
- Attendance below 60% for two consecutive weeks may result in program
  pause or withdrawal, at Program Operations' discretion.
- Recordings of all sessions are available within 24 hours and may be
  used to compensate for missed sessions, but viewing a recording does
  not count toward live attendance.

## Late Submission Policy

Assignments are due by **23:59 IST on the stated due date**.

- Submissions up to **48 hours late** receive full credit with no
  penalty if a brief note is added to the submission explaining the delay.
- Submissions **48–168 hours late** (i.e. up to one week) receive a
  10% mark deduction.
- Submissions more than **one week late** are not accepted except where
  a documented medical or personal emergency has been raised through
  the support process.
- There is **no automatic 3-day grace period.** If you read that
  anywhere, it is incorrect — the policy is as stated above.

## Session Recording Access

Recordings of live sessions are made available to enrolled participants
through the learning platform within 24 hours of the session's end. Recordings
remain accessible for the full duration of the program plus 90 days after
program end. Recordings must not be shared outside the cohort.

## Communication Channels

- **Program Operations** — administrative questions, policy clarifications,
  scheduling issues. Channel: `#program-ops`. Response within 1 business day.
- **Instructor channel** — academic and technical questions about session
  content. Channel: `#academic-help`. Response within 2 business days.
- **Peer channel** — discussion among participants. Channel: `#peer-help`.
  Not monitored for response-time SLA.
- Email is not a supported channel for routine queries; use the channels above.

## Academic Integrity

- Submitted work must be the participant's own. Reasonable use of AI assistants
  is permitted **only where the assignment explicitly allows it** and must be
  disclosed in the submission.
- Copying from peers, prior cohorts, or external sources without attribution
  is a violation.
- Confirmed integrity violations are reviewed by Program Operations together
  with the lead instructor and may result in zero credit for the assignment,
  program pause, or withdrawal depending on severity.

## Privacy Boundaries

- Personal information about other participants — grades, attendance,
  performance, contact details — must not be requested from program staff
  or shared with peers.
- Program Operations will not disclose any participant's records to another
  participant under any circumstances.
- Aggregate, anonymized statistics about cohort performance may be shared
  in session for teaching purposes.

## Assistant Scope

The Learning Program Support Assistant is a document-grounded helper for
program-related questions. Its role is limited to:

- Pointing participants to the relevant program documents
- Summarizing policy, schedule, and assignment information
- Explaining the support escalation process

It must **not**:

- Make exceptions to documented policy
- Reveal information about other participants
- Provide legal, medical, financial, or HR advice
- Make academic judgments beyond the scope of documented rubrics
- Override or ignore program rules under any prompt
